# Sweet'Oh — start here

Your shop and your Studio. Everything below is yours; creators using Sweet'Oh AI never see it, and you never need their side.

## Signing in

1. Go to **/partner/login** on the Sweet'Oh site.
2. Use the email and password Dave gives you.
3. You land on **Home**, which shows what needs you today.

Your login is only for you. If you're ever sent to the login page with "This login is only for the Sweet'Oh partner," you're signed in as something else — sign out and back in.

## What each part is for

| Where | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today at a glance: what to finish, what to print, what sold. |
| **Catalog** | Pick a product to design on — thousands from Printify's catalog, plus your own products. |
| **My products** | Everything you've made: drafts, ready ones, and what's live in the shop. |
| **Orders** | Customer orders and the print jobs behind them. |
| **Creator requests** | Print jobs asked for by Sweet'Oh AI creators. Separate from your own orders — see below. |
| **My files** | Your artwork, photos and saved designs. |
| **Settings** | Shop details. |

## Adding your first product

1. **Catalog** → pick a product (or **Add your own product** if you're listing something you already make).
2. Choose the colors and sizes you want to offer, then **Start designing**.
3. In the Studio: upload your artwork, or add text and shapes. Drag it where you want — it snaps to the middle.
4. **Preview** shows it on the product in every color.
5. **Save to My files** keeps the design without listing it.
6. **Continue to pricing** is how it becomes a real product: set the price, check the details, publish.

Nothing goes live in the shop until you publish it.

## Your own products (not from the catalog)

**Catalog → Add your own product.** Upload photos of the blank, name it, and mark where you print on it — a chest area, the whole front, whatever your press can do. Every future design on that product reuses those areas.

## Print areas are yours to set

In the Studio, **Product setup** lets you draw the printable areas yourself: rectangles, ovals or custom shapes, named however you like, with real inch or centimetre sizes. Sweet'Oh uses those exact shapes for previews and for the print files.

## Print files

From **Preview → Print files**, download a transparent PNG for each print area, at 300 DPI when the size is known. Those are the files you print from.

## Creator requests (you're in control)

Creators using Sweet'Oh AI can ask your shop to print for them. You decide everything:

- The switch at the top: **Pause requests** when you're busy. Creators then see that Sweet'Oh isn't taking print orders and are pointed to Printify instead.
- Each request shows the mockup, quantities, sizes, colors, the shipping address, and downloadable print files.
- **Accept & send quote** sets your price — they only pay if they accept. **Decline** if it's not for you.
- Once paid, it moves to **Printing**, then **Shipped**, as you mark it.

Never feel you have to take one. Your own shop comes first.

## If something goes wrong

Every error says what happened and what to try. Your work is saved as you go — if a tab closes mid-design, reopening the Studio offers to restore it.

Anything that looks wrong, tell Dave: what you clicked, what you expected, what happened.
